Day 1: Abundant Everything, Empty Something

Life looks fine. On paper, anyway. Steady job. Decent income. A social circle that’s… active, at least on the group chat. And yet. There’s this moment, usually late at night, when the noise fades and something surfaces. A quiet sense that a piece is missing. Not tragedy. Not crisis. Just a hollowness where something should be. This isn’t a personal failure. It’s a symptom of our time. A Generation’s Spiritual Symptom In the last fifty years, humanity generated more material wealth than in all previous centuries combined.
