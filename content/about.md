# About

## Our Mission

chan.surf exists to illuminate the rich tradition of Chinese Chan — the often-overlooked source of what the world calls Zen. We believe that understanding Chan's roots enriches our appreciation of its practice and philosophy.

## What We Do

We publish in-depth articles, series, and explorations of Chan history, philosophy, and practice. Our content is designed for both newcomers and those with some familiarity with Buddhist thought.

## About the Author

Written by Hu, a lifelong student of Chan and its place in the broader landscape of Buddhist philosophy.
