# Start Here

Welcome to chan.surf — your gateway to understanding Chinese Chan, the root of what the world knows as Zen.

## What is Chan?

Chan (禪) is the Chinese origin of Zen. While Zen has become widely known in the West, few realize it traces back to Chan, which emerged in China over 1,500 years ago. Chan represents a unique synthesis of Indian Buddhist meditation practices and Chinese philosophical traditions.
