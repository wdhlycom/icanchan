The Chinese source of Zen

Chan is not Zen.

Zen is the Japanese pronunciation. Chan is the Chinese original — the living root the West met only after it had already been trimmed, stylised, and renamed. We go back to the source, and we read it with modern eyes.

Start with Buddhism → Why science, not faith

What the West got wrong

The popular story

“Zen = sitting cross-legged, emptying the mind, a aesthetic of calm.”

Meditation became the only part of Buddhism the West could de-religionise and fMRI-scan. The rest got left behind.

What Chan actually is

Chan is one school of a whole tradition. Meditation is one of three trainings (ethics, concentration, wisdom) — and in Chan, the real practice happens standing, walking, eating, arguing.

Not “ Buddhism minus the religion.” The full architecture, read without the Japanese filter.

We don't ask you to believe. We show you.

No-self maps onto the brain's default mode network. Watching the breath reshapes the amygdala. Insight has measurable neural correlates. Chan made these claims fifteen hundred years before neuroscience could test them.
